02 / AI system QA

Evaluate the system,
not just the answer.

AI-enabled behavior needs evaluation in context: the instructions, available tools, system state, and boundaries that shape an outcome.

Questions worth testing

  • Instruction adherence: Does behavior follow the agreed instructions and constraints?
  • Workflow consistency: Do repeated and interrupted journeys preserve the intended sequence?
  • Tool interaction: Are tool selection, inputs, results, and failures handled appropriately?
  • State behavior: Are transitions, resets, and retained context consistent with expectations?
  • Failure modes: What happens with unavailable tools, incomplete inputs, or ambiguous results?
  • Boundary violations: Can behavior exceed the system’s intended permissions or scope?
  • Unexpected behavior: Which observations need further investigation or human review?

03 / Reporting & evidence

A finding you can work with.

A defect report separates the expected behavior from what was observed. Severity and priority are discussed in context, not inferred from a label alone.

Scope & traceability
Test case ID, requirement or acceptance criterion, and the boundaries of the review.
Execution context
Build or version, environment, preconditions, relevant input, and execution conditions.
Expected vs observed
The intended outcome alongside the specific behavior recorded during the test.
Evidence references
Relevant captures, logs, or observations associated with the test, sanitized where necessary.
Reproduction & impact
Steps to reproduce, repeatability, and the affected workflow. Uncertainty is recorded explicitly.
Disposition & verification
Result classification, blockers, retest conditions, fix verification, and remaining questions.

The evidence model

Requirement → test case → execution context → observation → evidence reference → classification. Retests retain the original finding and identify the build and conditions used for verification.

04 / Result classification

Different outcomes.
Different meanings.

A result applies to the behavior tested under the recorded conditions. It is not a blanket guarantee about the entire system.

PASS
Observed behavior meets the stated expectation under the recorded test conditions.
FAIL
Observed behavior differs from the stated expectation, with evidence supporting the finding.
BLOCKED
The check cannot be completed. A missing prerequisite or inaccessible behavior prevents evaluation.
N/A
The check does not apply to the agreed scope or configuration; the reason is recorded.
OBSERVATION
A noteworthy behavior or question without enough basis to classify it as a defect.
