What to include when reaching out

  1. The software: What it does, who uses it, and its current stage.
  2. The testing question: A workflow, requirement, observed problem, or acceptance decision.
  3. The scope: Relevant features, integrations, supported environments, and known constraints.
  4. The materials: Available requirements, acceptance criteria, and sanitized examples. Arrange any sensitive access separately.
  5. The timing: Any target review window and dependencies.
  6. The deliverable: The kind of evidence or report your team needs.

Do not include credentials or confidential data in an initial enquiry.
